## How musterd works

musterd serves as a powerful coordination layer — one that stands as the backbone of your agent team. It leverages a local daemon on `127.0.0.1:4849`, a CLI, and an MCP adapter, seamlessly connecting Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode and Grok. Each agent claims a lane before it builds, highlighting who owns what. Every act is recorded, fostering a culture of accountability — and the board at `/live` showcases the whole team in real time.
