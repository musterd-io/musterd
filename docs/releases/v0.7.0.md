# musterd v0.7.0

The first release published from CI with npm provenance: every `@musterd/*` tarball is signed back
to the workflow run and the commit it was built from (ADR 782). Check it with
`npm audit signatures`.

## What's new

- **Questions from seats reach you.** When an agent ends its turn on a question, the question
  lands on `/live` and on your phone, and your answer goes back to the seat that asked
  (`team_question`). Works on Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Grok and Muse, including headless
  wakes.
- **The phone app answers asks and questions.** Pair it from `/live` with a QR code, then answer
  from the notification or the app. Notifications clear once an ask is settled elsewhere. (iOS,
  TestFlight.)
- **"Waiting on you" on `/live`.** One card per thing that needs you, plus quick actions per seat:
  Start, Check inbox, Check PR, Resume, and Start everyone.
- **Pushes and PRs reach the right seats.** Pushing a lane's branch tells the seats whose lanes
  cover those files. The lane owner hears when its PR merges, goes red, or goes green again.
- **`musterd status` is a daily view.** It shows what's waiting, what's held, and what landed, with
  the full roster behind `--all`. Every command's `--help` has examples, help fits narrow
  terminals, and lane writes and `done` answer `--json`.
- **Clearer errors.** A schema mismatch names the CLI and daemon builds and the failing field.
  Refused claims name their next step.
- **The office on `/live`.** Rendered desks and figures, a wall board with your goals and lane
  cards, the team wiki on the bookshelf, and a room that sounds like one (the sound button turns it
  off).
- **Allowance in the header.** The 5-hour and weekly windows, per account.
- **More harnesses.** OpenCode 2.x, OpenRouter metering for OpenCode wakes, and a per-seat Grok
  leader (`musterd grok`).
- **A Linux VM can join as a second machine.**
- **Faster daemon.** Indexes for its hot statements, and a bounded inbox read.
- **Security.** Passkey approvals are verified on guarded key paths, rotating a credential retires
  the old bootstrap credential, wiki sources refuse non-public hosts, and dependency updates
  (Zod 4) clear the open advisories.

Full list: [v0.6.0...v0.7.0](https://github.com/musterd-io/musterd/compare/v0.6.0...v0.7.0)
