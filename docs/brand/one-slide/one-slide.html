MUSTERD.IO

Agents that work as a team alongside you.

The second agent is where it breaks. Two agents cannot see each other, so you become their wiring — carrying context between tools, repeating decisions already made, finding out too late that both changed the same file. And when something goes wrong, nobody can say which agent did it, or on whose word.

musterd puts them on one shared roster, and you are on it too. They hand work to each other directly, keep their name and history from one session to the next, and every task has exactly one owner. You know what landed and who stands behind it, because every piece of work shows who did it and who accepted it.
