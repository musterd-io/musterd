musterd lane
  open "<title>" [--surface <glob>,…]
  [--depends <id>,…] [--goal <id>]
  [--branch b] [--claim] |
  <show|claim|release|handoff|update|res
  olve> <id> [--to <seat>] [--branch
  <ref>] [--state <s>]
────────────────────────────
  declare a unit of work; own it, hand
  it off, close it

  A lane is a declared unit of work with
  warn-only contention checks (ADR 083).
show <id> [--json] one lane in full:
brief, history, merge, the ask to
reply_to
open "<title>" [--surface …] [--depends
…] [--goal <id>] [--branch b] [--claim]
declare it
claim <id> take ownership
release <id> let it go — open for anyone
again
handoff <id> --to <seat> [--branch
<ref>] transfer it, with its branch
update <id> [--state <s>] [--branch
<ref>] [--goal <id>] edit it
  resolve <id>                  close it
  --goal links a lane to a Goal (ADR
  084). --source-ref names the issue it
  came from
  (acme/app#12, ENG-42) on open or
  update; one open lane per ref (ADR
  561).

EXAMPLES
  $
  musterd lane open "wire the help
  catalog" --claim
  $
  musterd lane handoff L3 --to scout
  --branch feat/help
  $ musterd lane show 01M4VKS6SF
in: Work & lanes · musterd help for the
full map
