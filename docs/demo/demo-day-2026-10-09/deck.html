A day in the life of a member.

MMiley · designer · Claude Code

    Only the title is up. Each click adds one step and its caption; read the caption, then click. The inbox is the hub: it is checked after every tool call, a teammate's question arrives through it, and after Miley submits her work a tier 1 review request sends her to review someone else's, then back to the inbox. Wrap up is not the handoff act: a handoff transfers a lane, with its branch, to another member when she cannot finish it. "Ready for review" shows the flow we are moving to (open the PR, then review); today the merge comes first. Hooks named are Claude Code's; other harnesses get the same moments.
