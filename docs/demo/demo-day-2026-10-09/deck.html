MMiley · designer · Claude Code

    Only the title is up. Each click adds one step and its caption; read the caption, then click. The inbox is the hub: it is checked after every tool call, a teammate's question arrives through it, and after Miley submits her work a tier 1 review request sends her to review someone else's, then back to the inbox.
