# The goal bell — implementation plan

**Goal:** A brass bell over the work board replaces the shelf chimes. When a goal ships, the member who
recorded it walks over and rings it while the room turns and claps; a lane done is one soft ding and a
warm glow at the closer's desk.

**Architecture:** Four increments, each shippable on its own. (1) The chimes come out and the lane ding
and desk glow go in. (2) The bell is a rendered wall fixture with a live swing and its own sound, and
it rings by itself when a goal ships. (3) The figure gets two new poses. (4) The ceremony choreographs
the ringer, the room and the queue, using the poses from (3) and the bell from (2).

**Tech stack:** TypeScript canvas scene (`packages/web/src/live/office-scene/`), Web Audio
(`packages/web/src/live/sound*.ts`), Blender scripts (`packages/web/art/office/`), vitest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-goal-bell-design.md` (approved by nick 2026-11-03).

**Goal id:** `goal-bell`.

Each increment is one PR: `Lane \`<id>\`.` on its first line, auto-merge, then `lane_submit`.

## Global constraints

- No event or protocol change. The bell reads `walk-pin` with `state: 'done'` and `goal-done`
  (`mapping.ts`), as the chimes do; replays ring nothing.
- No chimes anywhere: not the object, not the `laneDone`/`goalDone` chime voices, not the stream's
  `chime('accept')` bar. Other cues keep their pitches.
- Sound is not motion: with reduced motion on, the bell still rings and the lane ding still plays.
- The ding is one note, at the closer's desk (`walk-pin`'s `who`), and must not sound like the
  question bell (two notes, rising).
- Budgets: `pnpm perf:check` (JS, CSS, `officeArtBytes`). Render loops stop when unseen.
- Fast gates only locally (`pnpm typecheck && pnpm lint && pnpm format:check`); tests run in CI.

## Review focus

1. **A goal ships while the tab is hidden, then the tab comes back.** No backlog of ceremonies plays on
   return: a ceremony older than 30 s is dropped. (Task 6 test.)
2. **The ringer is mid-walk on another errand** (a pin, a coffee run). The errand is cut short and the
   ringer walks from where they stand. (Task 7 test.)
3. **The ringer goes offline mid-ceremony.** The bell still rings at its time and the room still claps.
   (Task 7 test.)
4. **Six goals recorded in a burst.** Three ceremonies play in turn; the rest ring the bell alone, a
   strike each, spaced, never all at once. (Task 6 test.)
5. **A lane done for a closer with no desk** (a visitor, a bench seat). The ding plays centred on the
   board and no glow is drawn. (Task 1 test.)

### Task 1: Remove the chimes; one ding and a desk glow per lane done

**Files:**
- Modify: `packages/web/src/live/soundVoice.ts` (delete `CHIME_BARS`, `barForLane`, `voicesForChain`,
  `chordBars`), `soundEngine.ts` (`laneDone` becomes the ding; delete `goalDone`'s roll and the
  `done` branch of `chime()`), `soundLife.ts` (`chimeDelay` keeps only `laneDone`), and their tests.
- Modify: `office-scene/index.ts` (delete `chimeHits`, `strikeChimes`, `chimesSwinging`; the lane-done
  path calls the ding and the glow), `render.ts` (delete `shelfChimes`, `CHIME_SWING_S`, the `chimes`
  render option), `layout.ts` (delete `SHELF_CHIMES` and its test).
- Create: `office-scene/deskGlow.ts`, `deskGlow.test.ts`.

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `roomTone.moment('laneDone', pan)` with no `detail` (one note); `DING_HZ = 1046.5`
  (C6, a smaller, higher cousin of the bell's C5) in `soundVoice.ts`; `ringBell(ctx, out, at, gain)`
  in `soundVoice.ts` (used by Task 4), a long brass ring, synthesized: partials at 1, 2.0, 2.4, 3.0,
  4.2 × 523.25 Hz, decays 4.5 s to 0.8 s. Synthesized first; the spec prefers a recorded take
  (ADR 776), which can replace the voice later without touching any caller.
- Produces: `deskGlowAlpha(sinceS: number): number` (0 before, rising to 0.5 at 0.2 s, back to 0 at
  1.5 s) and `drawDeskGlow(ctx, fit, slot: DeskSlot, alpha: number): void` (a warm radial pool over
  the desk top, `'255, 206, 130'`).

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests.** `deskGlow.test.ts`: `deskGlowAlpha(-0.1) === 0`,
  `deskGlowAlpha(0.2) ≈ 0.5`, `deskGlowAlpha(1.5) === 0`, monotone down after 0.2.
  `soundEngine` test: a `laneDone` moment schedules exactly one oscillator note; `chime('accept')`
  schedules none. `layout.test.ts`: the chimes test is deleted, not skipped.
- [ ] **Step 2: Implement** the deletions and the ding; the glow paints in the stage pass (not the
  background bake) for 1.5 s after a closer's lane done, at `DESK_SLOTS` entry for their desk; no
  desk → no glow, ding panned to the board (Review focus 5).
- [ ] **Step 3: Fast gates; commit** `office: the chimes go; a lane done is a ding and a desk glow (lane 01M6HHH2JZ)`.

### Task 7: The ceremony on the floor

**Files:** `office-scene/index.ts` (the `goal-done` path now pushes to the queue), `actors.ts`
(`ringBell(from: string): boolean`, a walk to `BELL_STAND` and a `GESTURE.ring` hold),
`layout.ts` (`BELL_STAND`: on `BOARD_STAND`'s row, under the rope), `layout.test.ts`.

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `createCeremonies` (Task 6), `ringTheBell` (Task 4), `GESTURE.ring`/`GESTURE.clap`
  (Task 5), `actors.lookAt(name, at, { delay, hold })`, `actors.gestureBeat`.
- Produces: `actors.ringBell(from)`: walk at act-walk pace to `BELL_STAND`, face the wall, play `ring`,
  call back at the pull's bottom (`onPull`), walk home. Cancels the member's current errand
  (Review focus 2).

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests:** `BELL_STAND` is walkable and reachable from every desk
  (`nav.ts`), and clear of every seat's approach; `actors.ringBell` cancels an in-flight walk; when the
  ringer goes offline mid-walk, the scene still calls `ringTheBell` at the walk's planned time
  (Review focus 3).
- [ ] **Step 2: Implement.** On `next()` → `Ceremony`: if the ringer has a figure on the floor,
  `actors.ringBell`; everyone else present turns to the bell (`lookAt`, hold until the claps end) as
  the ringer stands; on `onPull`, `ringTheBell` and every looker plays `clap` for 2 s; then `done()`.
  No figure → `ringTheBell` at once, the room still turns and claps. `'strike'` → `ringTheBell` only.
  Reduced motion: no walk, no turn, no clap; `ringTheBell` at once, and the room's light warms for
  1.5 s (a `deskGlow`-style pool over the whole floor at alpha 0.12).
- [ ] **Step 3:** Check `/office-preview` with a fake `goal-done` (the preview's cue buttons) and on
  `/broadcast`.
- [ ] **Step 4:** Fast gates; PR for increment 4, auto-merge, `lane_submit`; then record the goal
  outcome on `goal-bell`.
